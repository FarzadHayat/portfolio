From December 2025 to January 2026 I was a Software Engineering Intern at **Tanda** in Brisbane. I was in a two-person team that designed and shipped a new feature for Tanda.

The feature is the **Compliance Document Library**. It is built with **Ruby on Rails** and gives businesses one secure place to store and manage their compliance documents. Staying compliant is hard, so the library tracks documents, updates their status automatically and gives business owners an easy way to manage them.

I worked on every stage, from design to release. I designed the database schema and used ActiveRecord to set up clear relationships and constraints, so the compliance data stays correct. I also built the automated email notifications. They tell people when a document's status changes, when one is about to expire, and when they need to act. That lowers the risk of missing something.

With only two of us, code quality mattered. I wrote unit and integration tests, took part in peer reviews and followed Rails conventions. A small team meant I took on a lot of responsibility and saw my code go from design to production quickly. I learned how much clear communication matters. Thanks to the Tanda team for the mentorship.
