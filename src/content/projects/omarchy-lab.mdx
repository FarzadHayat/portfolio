[Omarchy](https://omarchy.org) is an opinionated Arch Linux and Hyprland setup. I use it every day, and I keep building things that make it feel more like mine. [Omarchy Lab](https://omarchy.farzadhayat.dev) collects them so anyone can grab them, each with a one-line install.

## Projects

- **[Omarchy Pixel Cursors](https://github.com/FarzadHayat/omarchy-pixel-cursors):** animated 8-bit cursors that rebuild themselves in your theme's colors whenever you switch themes. [Try them in your browser](https://cursors.farzadhayat.dev).
- **[Omakeys Overlay](https://github.com/FarzadHayat/omakeys-overlay):** a floating live key map in Rust that shows what every key does on the layer you are on, including your real Hyprland binds.
- **[Omarchy New Tab](https://github.com/FarzadHayat/omarchy-newtab):** a keyboard-first new tab page for Chrome and Chromium that follows your Omarchy theme and wallpaper.
- **[Australia Theme](https://github.com/FarzadHayat/omarchy-australia-theme):** navy Southern Cross night, golden wattle and Uluru ochre.
- **[Liquid Glass Theme](https://github.com/FarzadHayat/omarchy-liquid-glass-theme):** an Apple Liquid Glass style theme with frosted see-through windows, squircle corners and a light rim.
- **[Omakeys](https://github.com/FarzadHayat/omakeys):** beginner-friendly QMK/VIA firmware for a 36-key keyboard, built to pair with the overlay.

## Upstream contributions

I've also had fixes and features merged into community Omarchy projects, including omarchy-spaces, omarchy-widgets, omamail, ai-usagebar and omamap.

## The site

A static site with no build step. It recolors live in all of Omarchy's stock themes, just like the desktop does, and remembers your choice.
